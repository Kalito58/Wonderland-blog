Difference of Life Before and After 2019

Thoughts about the internet, relationships, and why life feels different today.

Life before 2019, and especially before the COVID-19 pandemic, felt very different. The days seemed to move more slowly, life had a spark of never-ending happiness, and musicians released unforgettable songs that became part of our memories. In short, life felt calmer and less chaotic than it does today.

One major reason why life feels different today may be the way we use the internet. While the internet has made communication and learning easier, it can also trap our attention through endless scrolling, gaming, and social media.

How Internet Addiction Changes Life

Addiction is one of the biggest effects because many people spend hours scrolling through Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok, or playing games. This can reduce productivity, affect sleep, and make it difficult to concentrate.

Reduced Face-to-Face Interaction

Spending less time with people in person can affect individuals, families, and communities.

  • Weaker relationships
  • Loneliness
  • Poor communication skills
  • More misunderstandings
  • Mental health challenges
  • Weaker communities
  • Less teamwork experience
  • Slower social development in children

Finding Balance

The internet has brought incredible opportunities. The goal is not to avoid it completely, but to use it with discipline. Set screen-time limits, spend time outdoors, meet people in person, and remember that technology should support your life—not replace it.

"A healthy life is built by balancing the digital world with the real one."
